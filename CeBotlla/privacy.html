Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CeBotlla may handle information when you use the Bot on Discord.

1. Information we may process

Depending on the features you use and how CeBotlla is configured, the Bot may process information such as Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, command inputs, and server or user settings required to provide its features.

If you create or interact with tags or other user-generated content, the information necessary to provide those features may also be processed or stored.

2. Why information is used

Information may be used to operate commands, provide moderation and administration features, store configuration, provide tags and entertainment features, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and maintain the Bot.

3. Message content

CeBotlla does not require access to all messages in a server merely for the Bot to exist. However, certain commands or features may process message content or command arguments when necessary to perform the requested action.

4. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant feature, maintain configuration, prevent abuse, or fulfill another legitimate operational purpose.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the feature involved.

5. Data sharing

CeBotlla does not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to operate the Bot, comply with legal obligations, protect the service or its users, or address abuse and security issues.

6. Third-party services

CeBotlla operates through Discord. Your use of Discord remains subject to Discord’s own terms and privacy practices.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by CeBotlla. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

Depending on the type of information involved, you may request deletion of information associated with CeBotlla or ask questions about how your information is handled by contacting the developer through the official support channel.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. The latest version will always be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the developer through the official support channel or contact method provided by the project.

11. Discord

CeBotlla is a third-party application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord Inc.
